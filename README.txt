WEDDING INVITATION WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html
- styles.css
- script.js
- assets/

To preview:
Open index.html in a browser.

To edit:
- Names, date, address and schedule are in index.html.
- Colors, sizes and responsive layout are in styles.css.
- The RSVP form currently shows an on-page confirmation only. Connect it to Formspree, Google Forms, EmailJS or your own backend to store submissions.

Note:
Google Fonts are loaded online. Replace the font links with local files if you need the site to work fully offline.
